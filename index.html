Model Card: The Fair-Value Clienteling Engine

Architecture: BG/NBD (frequency/recency) + Gamma-Gamma (monetary spend) with 200 parallel Monte Carlo bootstraps.


Intended Use: Allocation of high-touch luxury clienteling services, VIP dedicated styling, and gifting budgets.


Audited Fairness Metrics: Disparate Impact Ratio (DIR) across geographic cohorts under the EEOC 4/5ths Rule (DIR ≥ 0.80).


Intersectional Finding: While aggregate international DIR passes (1.48), the mid-spend tier (£500-£2k) experiences an unlawful DIR breach of 0.6824 due to order consolidation friction.


Correction: Post-processing basket recalibration factor applied to VIP selection threshold. At λ=0.40, DIR reaches 0.8808 at +£2,793 spend gain. At λ=1.0, DIR reaches 1.324 at +£11,159.86 spend gain.
